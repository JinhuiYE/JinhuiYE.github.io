Figure 5 in the post gives one summary per benchmark. This page breaks results down by task families, suites, perturbation types or scenes, including VLA-Arena's L0–L2 levels. All results are from the GPT-6-Astra Native Policy behind starBenchServer.

How we count
  • Agents may stop voluntarily; each benchmark's native evaluator determines success.
  • Each table states its evaluation scope; results with different case totals are reported separately.
  • Groups follow the benchmark's own documentation where it has one. Groups marked (ours) are our own grouping by task name.
  • Most rounds cover a subset of the benchmark; the scope is given under each heading.
